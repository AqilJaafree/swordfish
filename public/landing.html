FAQ

01What is Swordfish?+

Swordfish is a strategy trading platform on HashKey Chain. It brings market data, technical analysis, backtesting, automated bots, and Telegram controls into one workflow.

02Can I try it without real funds?+

Yes. Every new bot starts on paper. You can replay strategies and follow simulated trades before choosing whether to go live. Paper performance does not guarantee future results.

03What strategies can I choose from?+

SMA Cross, MACD Trend, Breakout, RSI Reversion, Bollinger Reversion, Claude, and LP Range. Explore their logic and parameters in the dashboard’s strategy catalog.

04Does AI make every trading decision?+

No. Claude is one optional strategy. It reads indicators, pool prices, and trading costs to propose buy, sell, or hold decisions. Other strategies follow their own defined rules.

05How do I move from paper to live?+

Choose a bot and budget, then explicitly confirm the move to live trading in Telegram. Live swaps use supported V3 routes on HashKey Chain. The engine applies checks and records execution; liquidity strategies use a separate position-management path.

06How is the bot wallet managed?+

Swordfish uses a custodial bot wallet. Keys are encrypted at rest and decrypted in memory for signing. Telegram provides wallet management, export, and withdrawal commands.

07Where do I get started?+

Open the dashboard to explore markets and strategies. Sign in through Telegram, then deploy your first paper bot.
