# Landing artwork

## Current hero — original Swordfish artwork

`hero.mp4` and `hero-poster.jpg` are original renders made locally in Blender for Swordfish. The sculpture is modeled from the existing Swordfish SVG mark, with procedural titanium, chrome, graphite, and blue signal materials. No external models, textures, or footage are used in the current hero.

Editable source: `design/metallic-hero/swordfish-titanium.blend` and `design/metallic-hero/build_scene.py`. The video is an eight-second, 24 fps H.264 loop. The poster is its assembled opening frame.
